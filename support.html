Help with the Thrive app for iPhone

Contact

Email mrentrekin@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Where is my data?

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign in to or recover online. Deleting the app deletes its data, unless it's in an iPhone backup.

Importing from Apple Health

Open Track, then tap Import from Apple Health. To change what Thrive can read, go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Thrive.

Exporting for my care team

On Track, tap the share button. Choose spreadsheets for everything, or a short summary to send.

Removing the example entries

Open Profile (top right of Today) and tap Clear sample data.

In an emergency

Thrive is not a medical device. If you have a fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher during treatment, call your care team's urgent line. In an emergency, call 911. For emotional crisis support, call or text 988.

Thrive Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Thrive is a companion app for people living with cancer. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

What Thrive stores

Your check-ins, medicines and doses, appointments, vitals, journal entries, questions, care contacts and your first name. All of it is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.

What Thrive collects

Nothing. Thrive has no account and no server. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not share or sell any information. We cannot see what you enter.

Apple Health

If you choose to import from Apple Health, Thrive reads the types you allow (weight, body temperature, resting heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen and the medicines you choose to share) and saves copies on your iPhone. Thrive never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never sent off your device or used for advertising. You can change access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Exports

When you export spreadsheets or a summary, Thrive hands the file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (for example email or Files) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, Thrive's data is included in those backups under Apple's terms.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything Thrive stored. Profile → Clear sample data removes the example entries.

Children

Thrive is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
